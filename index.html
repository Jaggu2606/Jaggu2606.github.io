Robotics Projects

Multi-Skill Bipedal Robot with Learning-Based Control

Mar 2024 – Sep 2026 · M.Tech thesis, IIT Mandi

Designed a small-scale biped robot from the ground up, covering mechanical design, kinematic modeling and stability analysis. Trained locomotion policies with PPO, TD3 and SAC that achieve robust walking and running at over 90% success, using waypoint-based reward shaping to learn multiple skills on an underactuated platform.

Policies were developed in Gazebo, PyBullet and Isaac Gym and deployed on hardware through a ROS2 sim-to-real pipeline.

  • Reinforcement learning
  • Bipedal locomotion
  • ROS2
  • Isaac Gym
  • Sim-to-real

Hierarchical RL for Collision-Free Locomotion of an Underactuated Biped

Feb 2026 – Jul 2026

A two-level control architecture for obstacle-aware bipedal locomotion. A high-level waypoint policy reasons about the environment and proposes intermediate goals, and a low-level gait policy turns them into stable footsteps. Splitting navigation from gait generation lets an underactuated biped move through cluttered scenes without collisions.

  • Hierarchical RL
  • Navigation
  • Gait control

Autonomous Buried Power Cable Tracing Using Electromagnetic Locator Feedback

Jan 2025 – Jun 2026

A ground robot that follows buried power cables using feedback from an electromagnetic locator. It has no predefined goal and steers to keep the signal strong, with goal-free obstacle avoidance along the way. It also builds a GPS map of the cable route as it drives. The work runs alongside an autonomous pipe inspection system that uses computer vision for real-time navigation and defect detection.

  • Field robotics
  • Sensor fusion
  • Obstacle avoidance
  • GPS mapping

Deep RL for Vision-Based UAV Tracking by an Autonomous Ground Vehicle

Jan 2026 – Aug 2026

A vision-driven tracking policy for a heterogeneous UAV–UGV team, in which the ground vehicle learns with deep reinforcement learning to keep an aerial partner in view and follow it using onboard camera observations.

  • Deep RL
  • Computer vision
  • UAV–UGV teams
